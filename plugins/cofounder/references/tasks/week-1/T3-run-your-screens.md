# T3 — Run your screens

Two screens, merged, and deliberately **before** the founder loads any material. If
employer-owned material lands in the folder first and gets screened second, the damage is
already done.

Both screens end in flags, never clearance. Say that in those words.

## Gating questions

Ask both, one at a time:

> Did you work for someone in or near this space?

> Does this venture touch a regulated area, or other people's sensitive information —
> payments, health, children, legal or financial advice, biometrics, insurance?

Each answer branches. **A "no" is recorded, never silently skipped** — an attested n/a is
evidence; an absent section is a hole.

## Branch A — prior employer (if yes)

Ask these one at a time, then write the flags:

1. Have you signed an IP-assignment or proprietary-information agreement with a current or
   former employer?
2. Are you under a non-compete or non-solicit? What does it cover, and until when?
3. Have you used employer time, devices, accounts, or confidential information on this idea?
4. Is this idea related to your employer's actual or planned business?

Write results to `01-company/open-questions.md` under `## Legal flags — prior employer`.
Close with exactly:

> These are flags to raise with a lawyer, not a clearance.

If anything flagged: keep building this week, but do not put employer material into
`02-inputs/`, and tell the founder to put "talk to a lawyer" on their own calendar.

## Branch B — regulated vertical (if yes)

Screen against: payments or money movement; health data; users under 13; legal, medical,
financial or tax advice as the product; food, alcohol or cannabis; biometrics; insurance;
other people's personal data at scale.

Write hits to `01-company/open-questions.md` under `## Legal flags — regulated area`. For
each hit add: *licensing or counsel review needed before launch.* Close with the same line.

## The data-handling guardrail (gate, not advice)

This is a **condition of advancing**, not a footnote. Before T4 opens, the founder has
agreed in words to what does not go into `02-inputs/`:

- nothing from a current or former employer
- nothing under an NDA
- no one else's personal data they are not authorised to hold
- no health, card, or government-ID data

Say it, get an explicit acknowledgement, record it. If the venture's whole substance *is*
other people's personal data (recruiting, health, education), do not wave it through:
name the tension plainly, record it as a flag, and carry the constraint into T4 — the
distilled files describe the data, they do not copy it.

## When the screens are running late

Reconciliation over an existing folder hits this constantly: material is already in
`02-inputs/`, or already distilled, and the screens were never run. Anyone arriving from the
document version is in exactly this state. The ordering rationale — screen first so
employer-owned material never lands — has already been missed, and pretending otherwise is
worse than useless.

Handle it explicitly:

1. Say it plainly, once, without blame: *"These two checks were meant to come before you
   loaded your material, and they didn't. That's my document's fault, not yours. Running
   them now is still worth it — it just means we check what's already here as well."*
2. Run both screens as normal.
3. **Then check what is already in the folder against the result.** If the prior-employer
   screen flags anything, walk `02-inputs/` and the distilled files *with* the founder and
   ask, file by file, whether any of it came from the employer. Anything flagged: stop
   using it, note it in `open-questions.md`, and ask them whether to move it out of the
   folder — never move or delete it yourself.
4. If the distilled files quote flagged material, say which lines and offer to remove them.
   Their call, their file.

This is the single most consequential thing reconciliation does on a folder with existing work.
Do not shorten it.
