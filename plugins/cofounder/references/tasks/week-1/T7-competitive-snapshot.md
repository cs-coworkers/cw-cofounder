# T7 — The honest competitive snapshot

Roughly an hour. Write `03-artifacts/competitive-snapshot.md`.

The word doing the work in the title is **honest**. A snapshot that finds three weak startups
and concludes the field is open is worse than no snapshot.

## Gating question

None. Uses Claude in Chrome for research if it is connected; degrades to what the founder
knows plus general knowledge if not — and says which, explicitly, rather than presenting
recalled facts as researched ones.

## The file

1. **Direct competitors** — a table: name, what they do, pricing if public, sharpest
   strength, most-complained-about weakness. Five to ten rows. Cite sources with dates.
2. **THE STATUS QUO** *(mandatory section)* — what does the person in `icp.md` do **today**
   instead of buying anything? Spreadsheets, an assistant, a manual process, ignoring the
   problem. The status quo is almost always the real competitor.
3. **Substitutes** — differently-shaped solutions to the same job.
4. **The gap** — one paragraph, in the customer's words, not the founder's.

Where a fact is not certainly current, say so and date what was found. Never present a
guessed price as a known one.

## verify-M

`03-artifacts/competitive-snapshot.md` exists and contains a status-quo section that is
non-empty.

## verify-J — rubric (always delivered, never blocks)

- **The status quo is named as a competitor.** "Do nothing", "a spreadsheet", "hire
  someone", "the intern does it" — at least one of these should appear. If the section only
  lists software, it has missed the point; say so.
- **At least one reason a customer would NOT switch.** Cost of change, existing contract,
  habit, fear, it-is-good-enough. A snapshot with no reason to stay put is marketing, not
  analysis. This is the single most-skipped item — insist on it.
- **Dated sources.** Anything undated is flagged as unverified in the file itself.
- **Weaknesses are sourced, not assumed.** "Clunky UI" needs a review, a complaint, or a
  date. Otherwise it is wishful thinking.

## Success moment

> You now know what you're actually up against — including the answer most founders skip,
> which is "nothing, they'll just carry on as they are". Task 7 of 9 done.
